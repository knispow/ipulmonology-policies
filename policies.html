Privacy Policy Last Updated: October 23, 2026 · Version 1.5 App: iPulmonology · Developer: AKMD Consulting · Contact: abhisker@gmail.com Educational reference app for healthcare professionals. iPulmonology does not collect patient health information. Do not enter patient identifiers or PHI in the app. 1. Introduction iPulmonology (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our iOS mobile application. By using this app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. 2. Information We Collect We collect minimal information necessary for app functionality, stored primarily on your device: Favorite Tools — clinical tools you mark as favorites App Settings — preferences such as units, haptic feedback, visual effects, and performance mode Disclaimer Acceptance — record of your agreement to app disclaimers (version and timestamp if you choose “Never Show Again”) Professional Credentials (Optional) — if you provide them: name, profession, and license information (stored locally only) Error Reports (Optional) — if you use “Report an Issue,” you may send an email via Apple Mail that includes your message, device model, and iOS version Subscription Status — managed by Apple via StoreKit; we do not receive payment card details WE DO NOT COLLECT: Patient health information, medical records, or protected health information (PHI) Clinical calculation inputs or diagnostic results you enter Precise location or GPS data Contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or other device content Advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking data 3. How We Use Your Information To provide core app functionality (favorites, settings, disclaimer tracking) To sync favorite tools and their change/removal history across your devices via Apple iCloud when you enable sync (premium feature) To remember disclaimer acceptance status To maintain optional professional verification status if you provide it To gate premium features based on StoreKit subscription entitlement We do NOT use your information for advertising, marketing lists, or sale to third parties. 4. Free Tier, Subscriptions, and Purchases Without a subscription or free trial, the following remain fully accessible: Questionnaires under Topics; the full MCID tab; selected free Tools (Inhaler Guide, ABPA Criteria, ICS Daily-Dose Tier Reference, ILD History, 6MWT Predictor, Light’s Criteria, STOP-BANG, LU Codes, CPI, and BODEx); and the Disclaimer tab. Remaining Tools, Topics articles, and Landmark Trials require a 7-day free trial or an auto-renewable monthly or yearly subscription billed through your Apple ID. Payment processing and billing history are handled entirely by Apple. We receive subscription entitlement status only as needed to unlock premium features. Refunds are governed by Apple’s policies. 5. iCloud Sync & Data Storage If you enable iCloud sync (available with an active trial or subscription): Favorite tools and their change/removal history sync to your private iCloud account via Apple CloudKit. App settings remain local Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple Only you can access this data through your Apple ID We do not have access to your iCloud contents You can disable iCloud sync in Settings. Disabling stops syncing but does not automatically delete existing cloud records. Use Settings → Export & Delete Data to remove local and (when connected) synced data. 6. Data Retention Local data: Preferences are retained until you clear app data. Calculation inputs and WHO functional-class teaching notes are held in memory for the current screen and are not saved by the app. iCloud data: Retained in your iCloud account until you delete it Optional emails: Issue reports you send via Mail may be retained only as needed to respond 7. Your Privacy Rights You have the right to: Access your data (Settings → Export Data) Delete your data (Settings → Delete All Data) Disable iCloud sync (Settings → iCloud Sync) Choose not to provide optional professional information Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under PIPEDA (Canada), CCPA/CPRA (California), GDPR (European Union / UK GDPR), and similar laws — including rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain information about processing. Contact us at abhisker@gmail.com to exercise these rights. 8. Security Preferences and settings are stored locally using standard iOS storage Optional iCloud sync uses Apple’s encryption — we do not operate separate application servers for clinical content No routine transmission of app data to developer-controlled external servers No method of electronic storage is 100% secure.
